# Company Integrations & Multi-Dimensional Filters Walkthrough

## 1. Domain, Experience & Role Dropdown Filters (New Feature)

We have implemented dynamic multi-dimensional dropdown filtering across both the **Main Jobs Feed** (`/` and `/jobs`) and the **Company Open Roles Page** (`/companies/[id]`):

### Filter Dimensions Added:
1. **🏢 Domain / Function Dropdown**:
   - `All Domains / Functions`
   - `Technology & Software` (Engineering, Dev, Architecture)
   - `IT Infrastructure & Cloud` (ITIS, Cloud, DevOps, Networks)
   - `Business Process Services (BPS)` (Operations, Process Associates)
   - `Consulting & Enterprise Solutions` (SAP, Oracle, Infor, Business Consulting)
   - `Quality Assurance & Testing` (QA, SDET, Automation)
   - `Finance & Accounting` (Receivables, Payables, Accounts)
   - `Human Resources (HR)` (Talent, Recruitment, HR)

2. **🎓 Experience Level Dropdown**:
   - `All Experience Levels`
   - `Entry Level (0 - 2 Years)`
   - `Mid-Level (2 - 5 Years)`
   - `Senior (5 - 8 Years)`
   - `Lead / Principal (8+ Years)`

3. **💼 Role / Specialization Dropdown**:
   - `All Specializations / Roles`
   - `Software Engineer / Developer`
   - `Data & AI Engineer`
   - `Cloud & DevOps Engineer`
   - `Consultant / ERP Specialist`
   - `QA & Automation Engineer`
   - `Business / Financial Analyst`
   - `Operations / Process Associate`
   - `Architect / Tech Lead`

4. **🌐 Work Mode Dropdown**:
   - `All Work Modes`
   - `Remote`, `Hybrid`, `On-site`
